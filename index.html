About Me

Currently completing my master of engineering at UC Berkeley with a concentration in integrated circuits. My interests are RTL Design, Computer Architecture, System-Level Design, and Firmware. With solid experience in all these areas, I have a bird's eye view of the entire design process in making systems functional. My goal is to continue specializing in ASIC design, and build the most PPA-efficient chips that will power future applications.
