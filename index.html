About

Tofic is an electrical engineer and embedded-systems designer focused on instrumentation, firmware, and signal-processing pipelines for scientific applications. He has experience in analog and digital electronics, PCB design, FPGA/RTL development, and embedded firmware. He enjoys turning prototypes into reliable systems and collaborating across hardware and software boundaries.
